# Check My Words on an iPhone

Device checks are pending. Record the iPhone model, iOS version, Safari version, date, selected voice/language/rate, and results. Test with the intended user at her pace. She can use the app without trying to speak.

- [ ] Open the authorized HTTPS URL in Safari. Use Share → Add to Home Screen, launch the icon, and check the layout and safe areas. Opening a downloaded HTML file from Files is unsupported.
- [ ] Use the left hand in portrait and landscape. Choose I → Want → phone through the separate pages, then speak from the Message page. Check reach, accidental taps, and button spacing.
- [ ] Skip each builder step and move between numbered pages. Check that words and the visible message are kept. Choose Other on Who, select a saved person or Someone else, and finish a message. Browse Topics, point with That, return from categories, and choose a favorite. Confirm daily communication has no typing or alphabet board.
- [ ] Enable VoiceOver. Check labels, selected states, message announcements, and back buttons. Enlarge text/zoom. Check focus and speech controls remain reachable without sideways scrolling. In settings, open the keyboard and check you can still move between controls.
- [ ] Check Yes, No, and Quick words stay at the bottom on every page and in settings. Yes, No, Wrong, Help, Pain, Done, and both conversation starters must show and speak their message when tapped. After a shortcut starts speaking, reopen Quick words and tap Stop speaking. Check this is easy to reach. Check VoiceOver announcements do not make speech confusing.
- [ ] On the Who page, select Not before choosing words. Check it changes the preview silently. On the Message page, check Undo, Clear, and Restore message without unwanted speech.
- [ ] Check Speak and Repeat use exactly the displayed message and Stop speaking stops promptly. Tap several speech controls quickly; check for overlap or a growing queue. Change voice, language, and rate. Check delayed voice loading and visible errors without losing the message.
- [ ] Wait for Ready for offline use, close Safari/app, disable Wi-Fi and cellular, and relaunch each copy. Compose and browse personal photos. Test the chosen voice separately; caching the app does not guarantee offline speech.
- [ ] Save a custom word, photo, favorite, and settings. Close and reopen the Home Screen copy. Confirm they remain. Check Safari separately and import a backup if needed.
- [ ] Upload a camera/library photo and edit its label. Download a backup to Files, verify the file exists, and restore it into a fresh test copy. Check merge and replace, including labels, photos, settings, and favorites. Convert HEIC to JPEG if needed.
- [ ] With an older installed version and a new authorized version available, compose a message. Confirm no forced reload. Clear when finished, apply the offered update, and verify personalization remains.

Save a backup before clearing storage. Record any unsupported voice, upload format, or device behavior before relying on the app for daily use.
